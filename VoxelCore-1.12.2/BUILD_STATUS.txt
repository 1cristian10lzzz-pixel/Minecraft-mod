0.7.1 changes:
- Player animation model installed before vanilla pose calculation; subtle secondary motion is applied afterwards. Default/slim skin geometry and overlay parts are retained.
- Ground item renderer explicitly binds Minecraft's block atlas, draws one reduced-size baked model per dropped stack, and retains the model's GROUND transform.
- Server-side pickup recovery calls EntityItem.onCollideWithPlayer so Minecraft still enforces pickup delay, inventory capacity and item transfer.
- Nearby water surface checks have a shorter radius and interval; ripple lines use 40 segments in normal quality and keep the low-FPS fallback.
- Liquid buoyancy scans are throttled to every fifth world tick.
- Existing PNG assets are unchanged; no images were created, edited or added.
- The internal mod ID remains "voxelcore"; Minecraft/Forge versions, item IDs and block IDs are unchanged.

A passing Forge build and asset validation do not prove actual in-game behaviour. A Minecraft client test and multiplayer/server pickup test are still required.
