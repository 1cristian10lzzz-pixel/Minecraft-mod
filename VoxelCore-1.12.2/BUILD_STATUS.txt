0.8.0 changes:
- Custom calm surface mesh over exposed Minecraft water with shared smoothed corner heights.
- Biome/depth colour absorption, sky reflection approximation, view-angle Fresnel response, daylight-dependent specular highlights and shoreline foam.
- No geometric wave displacement and no expanding circular ring geometry. Reflections/highlights and foam brightness can change without moving the surface.
- Underwater colour absorption and depth-based fog, rising bubbles, local splashes/wakes and waterfall mist.
- Water remains vanilla in block identity, flow, level, collision, bucket interactions, world state and server simulation; Minefade changes client-side presentation only.
- Dropped-item buoyancy uses gentler drag/lift and damping near the surface to reduce bobbing jitter.
- Swimming animation includes alternating leg kicks.
- No GUI or interface files changed. No images or textures created, modified or added.
- Mod ID remains "voxelcore"; Minecraft/Forge version and existing item/block registry IDs remain compatible.

Important rendering limit:
The custom surface uses procedural vertex geometry and a sky-colour/specular approximation. It does not provide full screen-space reflections, true refraction or a physically based fluid simulation. Those require full shader/framebuffer or chunk-renderer integration and carry greater Forge 1.12.2 compatibility risk.

A passing Forge build and asset validation do not prove actual in-game behaviour. Client rendering and dedicated/LAN runtime tests remain required.
