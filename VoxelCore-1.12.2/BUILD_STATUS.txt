0.8.0 changes:
- New batched custom water surface overlay with animated procedural mesh, biome/depth colour, Fresnel-like sky reflection and specular approximation, shore foam and adaptive low-FPS mesh detail.
- Old expanding ripple-ring renderer removed; no ring geometry is used as a substitute for the water surface.
- Underwater colour absorption and depth-based fog, rising bubbles, splashes, wakes and waterfall mist retained/refined.
- Water remains vanilla in block identity, flow, level, collision, world state and server simulation; Minefade changes the visible surface and underwater presentation only.
- Dropped-item buoyancy uses gentler drag/lift and damping near the surface to reduce bobbing jitter.
- Swimming animation now includes alternating leg kicks.
- No GUI or interface files changed. No images or textures created, modified or added.
- Mod ID remains "voxelcore"; Minecraft/Forge version and existing item/block registry IDs remain compatible.

Important rendering limit:
The custom surface uses procedural vertex geometry and a sky-colour/specular approximation. It does not provide full screen-space reflections, true refraction or a physically based fluid simulation. Those require a full shader/framebuffer or chunk-renderer integration and would carry substantially higher Forge 1.12.2 compatibility risk.

A passing build and asset validation do not prove actual in-game behaviour. Client rendering and dedicated/LAN runtime tests remain required.
