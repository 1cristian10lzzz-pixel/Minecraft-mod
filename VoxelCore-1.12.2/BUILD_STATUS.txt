0.7.0 source changes:
- Player animation model is installed in RenderPlayerEvent.Pre, before vanilla calls setRotationAngles; the subclass adds secondary motion after vanilla calculates the base pose.
- Slim/default skin arm geometry and skin overlay rotations are preserved.
- Ground item renderer explicitly binds the vanilla block atlas and draws one smaller baked model per dropped stack to reduce oversized piles and draw calls.
- Server-side player tick recovery delegates pickup to EntityItem.onCollideWithPlayer, preserving vanilla inventory capacity and pickup rules.
- Water surface scans run less frequently and search a smaller local area; ripple rings use more segments with adaptive low-FPS reduction.
- Fluid buoyancy work is throttled to every fifth world tick.
- Existing texture PNGs are not modified, added or regenerated.
- Mod ID remains "voxelcore"; Minecraft/Forge target and content registry IDs are unchanged.
